About Me

The Designer Who Ships

Most designers design. Most developers develop. I do both — and I obsess over the bit in the middle where products actually succeed or fail.

San Francisco, 2024 — building between cities

My obsession with technology started back when the era of personal computers began — I've been a deep tech geek ever since. I started professionally building on the internet in 2012 — logos, websites, whatever clients needed. Over 12+ years, that curiosity compounded into a rare hybrid skill: I can sit with a founder, understand what they're trying to build, design the experience, write the code, and iterate until users love it.

I've been Co-Founder of two products (Allsteps.ai, Farmsnation), lead designer at an AI SaaS startup (Emendo), and the person behind high-converting Shopify stores for fashion and luxury brands in New York, LA, and Europe.

Today I use AI-accelerated workflows (Claude, Cursor AI) to compress timelines without cutting corners — shipping production-quality products in a fraction of the traditional time, giving clients a genuine competitive edge.

I'm based in India, working globally with ambitious founders and growing brands who want a partner, not just a vendor.
